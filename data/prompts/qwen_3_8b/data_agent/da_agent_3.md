Final Report

Based on the analysis of the Warehouse_and_Retail_Sales.csv dataset, we have identified several key insights and trends.

Channel Mix

The majority of sales come from warehouse transfers (85.12%), followed by retail (7.49%) and transfers (7.39%).
This suggests that the company’s distribution strategy is heavily focused on warehouse operations.
Supplier Concentration

The top 10 suppliers account for approximately 53.29% of total units sold.
CROWN IMPORTS, MILLER BREWING COMPANY, and ANHEUSER BUSCH INC are the largest contributors to this concentration.
ITEM TYPE Mix

Among the top 5 suppliers, BEER is the dominant ITEM TYPE for all except E & J GALLO WINERY (which has a mix of WINE and LIQUOR).
Anomalies

There were 61425 anomaly rows in the dataset, with the majority being retail-only sales.
Warehouse spikes accounted for approximately 9% of total units sold.
Trend Analysis

The trend analysis revealed that sales have been increasing over time, with a significant spike in 2020 Q3.
BEER sales have consistently been the highest among all ITEM TYPES.
Recommendations

Optimize Warehouse Operations: Given the high share of warehouse-led sales, it may be beneficial to optimize warehouse operations, including inventory management and shipping logistics.
Targeted Marketing: Suppliers with balanced persona distributions (e.g., CROWN IMPORTS) may benefit from targeted marketing campaigns to increase sales in underrepresented channels.
Supplier Development: The high concentration of sales among top suppliers suggests opportunities for supplier development programs to help smaller suppliers grow their market share.
Action Items

Review and optimize warehouse operations to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
Develop targeted marketing campaigns for suppliers with balanced persona distributions.
Implement supplier development programs to support growth of smaller suppliers.
By implementing these recommendations, the company can improve its sales performance, increase revenue, and enhance its market competitiveness.
